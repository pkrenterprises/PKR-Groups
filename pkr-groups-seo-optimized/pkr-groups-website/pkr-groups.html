PKR Groups — Businesses, Brands, Directors & Sectors

PKR Groups is a diversified business ecosystem covering enterprise, technology, agriculture, mobility, commerce, water and beverages, and social initiatives.

The PKR Groups ecosystem includes PKR Enterprises, VAYUPRABA Technologies, SHRIVAINA, KSHITRIVA, KR Nurseries, AKSHIRA, AMRIT, SARVAMPADA, SHEEGRA and MSR Charitable Trust & Foundation.

Founder & CEO: M. Prasanna Kumar Reddy. The group maintains dedicated pages for its directors, brands and sectors so visitors and search engines can understand each part of the ecosystem.
